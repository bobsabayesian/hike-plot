# Roadmap

Ideas for making the poster's landscape less plain, roughly in order. Done items move to the bottom.

## Next

- **Unhide a hidden landmark**: a list of the landmarks hidden by hand, each with a button to show it again.
- **A solid base under the 3D terrain**: cut the model at a floor and drop vertical lines from its front and side
  edges, like a museum terrain model, with optional strata lines on the cut faces. Hides the ragged outline.
- **More for the route in 3D**: a "curtain" of short drop lines from the route to the floor (or its shadow on the
  floor), mile or km ticks along it, and a summit marker with its elevation at the high point.
- **Lighter lines in the distance**: thin the rows toward the back, or move them to a lighter pen, so the near slopes
  come forward.
- **Distant ridges**: the skyline beyond the view, from a wider elevation grid, as one faint line in a light pen.

## Done

- **Land cover** (Land cover section): forests, rock and scree, glaciers and snow, meadow, wetland and sand from the
  OpenFreeMap tiles, each filled with a plotter pattern (trees, dots, hatching, scree dashes, marsh tufts) or left as
  clear paper. Drawn on the flat map and draped on the 3D relief.
- **Slope shading** (Shading section): slopes facing away from the sun drawn darker, with extra lines between them in
  the shade.
